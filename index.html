Frequently asked questions

Where do StockUp's prices come from?

From publicly available Coles and Woolworths data, collected independently every week. StockUp isn't affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by either supermarket. Read exactly how we collect and match prices →

Is StockUp free?

Yes. Comparing prices, browsing half-price specials, price-drop alerts, your List and Cart, and barcode scanning are all free. Premium adds the power features in the table above.

Do you sell my data?

No. We don't sell or rent your personal information, and you can delete your account (and everything with it) in the app at any time. The details are in our privacy policy.

Does StockUp show ads?

The free version is supported by ads. StockUp Premium removes them. Either way, Home, your Cart and your profile stay ad-free. What that means for your data.

What about ALDI or IGA?

IGA stores are independently owned, so prices differ from store to store - there's no single IGA price to compare against. ALDI doesn't publish its prices online, and most of its range is ALDI's own brands, so there's little to compare like-for-like. We'd rather cover Coles and Woolworths properly than add chains we can't price accurately.

Do I need an account?

No sign-up needed - open the app and start comparing straight away. An account is optional, for syncing across devices or subscribing to Premium.

What's in StockUp Premium?

Full Price History, Price Intelligence (our buy-now-or-wait read on every product's sale pattern), Smart Shop for live prices and stock while you're in the store, one-tap Quick Cart trolley fill, Past Purchases - your savings ledger - and an ad-free experience. $4.99/month or $39.99/year. Cancel anytime in your store account.

What is Smart Shop?

Smart Shop is for when you're in the supermarket, or heading there. Pick the store and StockUp pulls that store's live prices and stock, then turns your shop into a checklist you tick off as you go. When you finish, the shop is saved to Past Purchases. It's part of StockUp Premium.

How does Quick Cart work?

You sign in to Coles or Woolworths inside the app - on your device, on their website - and StockUp adds your Cart's items to your real trolley at whichever store is cheaper. You review and check out as normal. StockUp never sees your password and never places orders.

Does StockUp have a shopping list?

Yes. Your Cart is StockUp's shopping list: add this week's shop and it splits between Coles and Woolworths by whichever store is cheaper for each item. There's also your List - the products you save so StockUp can watch their prices and alert you when they drop.

How accurate are the prices?

They're the retailers' standard national prices, refreshed every Wednesday when the new catalogues start, so the odd store can vary from what you see. When you're actually in a store, Smart Shop pulls that store's own live prices and stock.

Does StockUp work outside Australia?

No. StockUp is built for Australian grocery shopping and covers Coles and Woolworths only. It's available on iPhone and Android in Australia.
