Frequently asked questions

Where do StockUp's prices come from?

From publicly available Coles and Woolworths data, collected independently every week. StockUp isn't affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by either supermarket - we're the independent referee. Read exactly how we collect and match prices →

How often are prices updated?

Prices at both chains move on Wednesdays, when the new catalogues start - so that's when StockUp refreshes every price and sends your alerts. Smart Shop adds live store prices while you're physically shopping.

Is StockUp free?

Yes. Comparing prices, browsing half-price specials, price-drop alerts, your List and Cart, and barcode scanning are all free. Premium adds the power features in the table above.

Do you sell my data?

No. We don't sell or rent your personal information, and you can delete your account (and everything with it) in the app at any time. The details are in our privacy policy.

Does StockUp show ads?

The free version is supported by ads - in search results, category browsing, the List tab and product pages. Home, your Cart and your profile stay ad-free for everyone. StockUp Premium removes ads everywhere. We use Google AdMob; see our privacy policy for what that means for your data.

What about ALDI or IGA?

ALDI doesn't publish its prices online, and IGA pricing varies store to store - so no app can compare them accurately. We'd rather cover Coles and Woolworths deeply - history, alerts, trolley fill - than five chains shallowly.

Do I need an account?

No sign-up needed - open the app and start comparing straight away. An account is optional, for syncing across devices or subscribing to Premium.

What's in StockUp Premium?

Full Price History, Price Intelligence (our buy-now-or-wait read on every product's sale pattern), Smart Shop for live prices and stock while you're in the store, one-tap Quick Cart trolley fill, Past Purchases - your savings ledger - and an ad-free experience. $4.99/month or $39.99/year. Cancel anytime in your store account.

What is Smart Shop?

Smart Shop is for when you're actually in the supermarket. Pick the store you're standing in and StockUp pulls that store's live prices and stock, then turns your shop into a checklist you tick off as you go. When you finish, the shop is saved to Past Purchases. It's part of StockUp Premium.

How does Quick Cart work?

You sign in to Coles or Woolworths inside the app - on your device, on their website - and StockUp adds your Cart's items to your real trolley at whichever store is cheaper. You review and check out as normal. StockUp never sees your password and never places orders.

Does StockUp have a shopping list?

Yes. Your Cart is StockUp's shopping list: add this week's shop and it splits between Coles and Woolworths by whichever store is cheaper for each item. There's also your List - the products you save so StockUp can watch their prices and alert you when they drop.

How accurate are the prices?

Prices are collected from publicly available Coles and Woolworths data and refreshed every Wednesday, in step with the specials cycle. They're the retailers' standard national prices, so a handful of stores may vary. When you're actually in a store, Smart Shop pulls that store's live prices and stock.

Does StockUp work outside Australia?

No. StockUp is built for Australian grocery shopping and covers Coles and Woolworths only. It's available on iOS and Android in Australia; there's no web version.
