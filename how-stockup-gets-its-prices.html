How StockUp gets its prices

StockUp is a free Australian app that compares grocery prices at Coles and Woolworths side-by-side, tracks price history, sends price-drop alerts, and splits your shopping by whichever store is cheaper. Every number in the app rests on the process described on this page.

Where the prices come from

StockUp's prices come from publicly available Coles and Woolworths data, collected independently by us. We collect every price ourselves, the same way a very patient shopper with a very large notebook would. StockUp isn't affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by either supermarket - we're the independent referee between them, and neither chain has any say in what the app shows.

When prices update

Prices at both chains move on Wednesdays, when the new weekly catalogues start. That's when specials begin and end, so that's when StockUp refreshes every price and sends price-drop alerts. A daily refresh would look busier but tell you nothing new - the prices that matter change once a week, and we're synchronised to that cycle. The one exception is Smart Shop: when you're physically in a supermarket, it pulls that store's live prices and stock from the retailer's own systems, so what you see in the aisle is current, not Wednesday's snapshot.

How much we cover

StockUp tracks 30,000+ products matched across both chains - the everyday groceries Australians actually buy, from milk and bread to laundry liquid and dishwasher tablets. Every product shows per-unit pricing, so a 575g box and a 1kg box compare fairly instead of by sticker price.

How products are matched

To compare a product across both chains we first have to be confident it's the same product. Matching uses barcodes and product data - brand, name, and pack size. When we can't match a product with confidence, we show it per-retailer rather than guess: a wrong match would produce a wrong saving, and a wrong saving is worse than no comparison.

Our honesty rules

Three rules govern every savings number StockUp shows, and we hold ourselves to them publicly:

  1. In the app, your saving is measured against the better single-store shop, never the dearer one. Comparing your split cart against the more expensive store would inflate the number, and you'd have picked the cheaper store anyway. Where we show a range in our marketing, both single-store totals are on screen beside it, so you can see exactly which comparison a number is using.
  2. Example baskets are never reconstructed to inflate numbers. The basket on our homepage is an illustrative weekly shop built from a real week of half-price cycles, and both chains win items in it - because that's how real weeks work.
  3. Prices shown are the retailers' standard national prices. A handful of stores vary from those prices, which is exactly why Smart Shop exists for the store you're standing in.

What we don't cover, and why

ALDI doesn't publish its prices online, and IGA pricing varies store to store - so no app can compare either of them accurately, and we'd rather be silent than wrong. StockUp covers Coles and Woolworths deeply - history, alerts, cart splitting, trolley fill - instead of covering five chains shallowly.
