Smart Shop

StockUp Premium includes a "Smart Shop" feature for shopping in person. When you start a shop you select the retailer and the specific store you are visiting, and the App requests that store's prices and stock availability from the retailer's own systems.

Live prices and stock availability are indicative only. They are supplied by the retailer, reflect that retailer's own records rather than the physical shelf, and may be out of date, incomplete, or unavailable. An item shown as in stock may not be on the shelf, and an item shown as out of stock or not stocked may in fact be available. Where live data cannot be retrieved, the App shows estimated prices and labels them as such. Always verify prices and availability in store - the price at the register is the price you pay.

Finishing a shop saves a record of it to your purchase history unless you choose not to record it; see our Privacy Policy. We may disable Smart Shop for either or both retailers at any time, with or without notice, including if a retailer changes the way their systems work.
